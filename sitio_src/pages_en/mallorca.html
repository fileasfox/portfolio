What is a sea view worth in Mallorca?

Which features of a holiday rental justify a higher price, once size, area, property type and season are taken into account. It is the same question a hotel asks when it prices a room with a view or an upgrade.

From raw files to a business answer

The answer

For the same size, area, type and season, a sea view is associated with a 27% higher price, a pool with 19% and air conditioning with 12%.

The trap

Comparing medians without controls, a pool seems to be worth +73%. That figure overstates the feature almost four times.

What data, and what problems it had

Inside Airbnb, Mallorca, scraped on 23/06/2026, CC BY 4.0 licence.

25.4% without a price, and not at random

The 3,760 listings without a price average 2.6 reviews in the last year, against 6.9 for those with one, and 48% had no activity. They are barely active listings: they are excluded from the model, and that is stated (query 03).

The price is a quote

Each price refers to specific check-in dates between June and January. Without controlling for month, the model would confuse season with features.

“Pool” is not always a pool

With a free-text search, “pool table” and “pool view” counted as a pool, and 5,482 listings say just “pool” even when it is private. The fix was to read the amenities list item by item.

Prices up to €22,260

For the model I use the 1st to 99th percentile range (€71 to €3,870) and the log of price, so luxury villas do not dominate.

The code that does the work

Excerpts from analisis_mallorca.py, simplified to read here. Each tab explains what the code does and why I wrote it that way. Identifiers are in Spanish, as in the original project.

What it does

Converts price from text to a number, computes the percentiles used to trim outliers and prepares the model sample.

Why this way

errors="coerce" stops an odd value from bringing the process down. The log turns coefficients into price percentages.

What it does

Reads each listing's amenities list and creates a 0/1 variable per feature.

Why this way

The first version searched free text and counted “pool table” as a pool. By evaluating each item separately, with anchors and a negative lookahead, the pool premium went from −5.5% to +19.1%.

What it does

Aggregates by municipality with several metrics at once, bands the host portfolio and counts reviews per year and month.

Why this way

With agg and explicit names, the table comes out ready to publish. Median income excludes zeros so inactive listings are not mixed in.

What it does

Builds the matrix with features and controls (dummy variables for municipality, type and month) and estimates the regression with robust errors.

Why this way

I coded it with numpy instead of a library to understand every step. HC1 errors do not assume the same variance for villas and apartments.

The same questions, in SQL, and reconciled

I loaded the raw files into SQLite and ran 8 queries. What you see below are the queries and the real results of that run. I then computed the key figures in pandas as well and compared them.

Why reconcile

In my job, two reports with two different figures mean nobody trusts either. That is why I compute every key figure two ways before publishing it.

Price premium by feature

Regression on the log of price. Sample: 9,255 entire homes. Controls: capacity, bedrooms, bathrooms, municipality, property type and check-in month.

The analyst's lesson

Listings with a pool have a median of €488 against €282, 73% more. With controls, the premium is 19%: most of the gap is explained by homes with pools being larger and being villas.

    What the market looks like

    What a business would do with this

    Hotel

    Use the ≈ 25% sea-view premium as a reference ceiling for the upgrade to a room with a view, and sell air conditioning or the pool as separate features.

    Holiday rental manager

    Price by feature and by check-in month, not by the area median. The gap between the raw and the controlled figure avoids overvaluing a house.

    Destination

    Track how concentrated supply is and how much depends on summer, to plan services and regulation.
