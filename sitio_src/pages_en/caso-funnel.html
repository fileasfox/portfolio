Invitation funnel and integration alerts

Bookings arrive from each hotel's system (PMS or booking engine) through an API. If the connector fails, invitations are not sent and the hotel loses sales without knowing it. You need to tell whether the problem is commercial or technical.

SQLREST APIsPMS and booking enginesFunnels

The funnel

  1. Imported: the booking arrives from the hotel's connector.

  2. Notified, or not notified with a reason (no email, language, hotel rules).

  3. Email sent, delivered, opened and clicked.

  4. Entry: the guest enters the room-selection flow.

  5. Request for a room or upgrade.

  6. Handling by the hotel: assigned, pending or rejected.

If the funnel drops from the click onwards while imports are normal, the problem is commercial: visibility, prices or messaging. That goes to the accounts team.

Result

A daily review that separates a commercial drop from a technical one and assigns it to whoever can fix it. Crossed with each hotel's tech stack (booking engine, PMS), failures group by supplier and patterns show up earlier.

Where it applies

Any product that depends on integrations (e-commerce, fintech, marketplaces): telling whether it is the business or the data pipeline that is falling.
