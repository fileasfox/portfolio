About me

I hold a BA in Tourism from the University of the Balearic Islands and have spent almost two years at Hotelverse, a Palma start-up that lets guests choose their exact room before they arrive at the hotel.

The product (Choose Your Room and Attribute-Based Selling) is live in more than 80 international hotel chains. I started out coordinating hotel roll-outs and solving integration issues with their systems. Today, as Performance Manager, my job is to measure whether each hotel is getting value from the product and to act when it is not.

Day to day I build views in BigQuery and reports in Looker Studio, spot commercial and technical drops before the client notices them, and present results to senior management in quarterly reviews. Before I treat a figure as final, I reconcile it against a known reference.

I am comfortable working across three areas that rarely go together: the hotel business, data, and the technical side (APIs, PMS, booking engines and Google Tag Manager). I am looking for a Data Analyst, BI or Customer Success Analytics role where data turns into decisions.
